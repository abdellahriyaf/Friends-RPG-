07 / EQUIPMENT & BUILDCRAFT

BUILD A LOADOUT, NOT A GUESS.

Understand the equipment layers before spending rare materials.

⚔

WEAPONS

Simply Swords adds weapon variety; Better Combat changes how compatible melee weapons feel. Compare reach, attack speed, special effects and your class skills.

⬡

ARMOR

Vanilla armor remains a dependable baseline. Modded armor and Mythic Metals gear add more paths, but the best choice depends on stats, effects and recipe cost.

✧

ARTIFACTS & TRINKETS

Accessories can add effects that armor alone cannot. Check available slots, item descriptions and whether effects stack before choosing a set.

⌁

MAGIC & SPELLS

Spell Engine and Spell Power support spellcasting. Match spells to your role and balance damage, range, cooldowns and survival.

Important: This guide intentionally avoids inventing a universal “best armor” or exact weapon damage ranking. Those values must be extracted from the exact installed JARs and live configs. Use REI and in-game tooltips for the authoritative recipe and stat display.
